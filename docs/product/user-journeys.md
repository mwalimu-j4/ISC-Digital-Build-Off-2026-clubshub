# User journeys

The journeys below describe intended outcomes, not implemented workflows.

## Discovering a community

1. A student arrives at ClubsHub.
2. They scan interest categories and understand the value of joining.
3. They select a category and compare clubs.
4. They choose a future club detail experience.

## Finding a campus event

1. A student opens Events from the main navigation.
2. They scan dates, location, and hosting club.
3. They open a future event detail page.

## Returning a lost item

1. A student opens Lost & Found.
2. They browse found items or begin a future report.
3. They coordinate a safe handoff through a future, moderated flow.

## Managing a club (future)

An authorized club administrator will eventually manage club information and activity
from a role-restricted workspace. No management workflow is included in Stage 1.
