# User personas

These lightweight personas express design hypotheses for validation. They are not
claims about researched individuals.

## The exploring student

Wants to find people with shared interests without already knowing which clubs exist.
Needs clear categories, welcoming language, and useful event context.

## The busy student

Wants to quickly scan what is happening this week and save time finding the right
place for updates. Needs readable hierarchy, responsive layouts, and low-friction
navigation.

## The club administrator

Needs a future, role-restricted workspace for representing a club and coordinating
activity. Stage 1 only accounts for this future role in the information architecture.

## The super administrator

Needs a future governance and moderation workspace with strong auditability. Stage 1
does not implement or expose administrative operations.
