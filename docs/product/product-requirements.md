# Product requirements

## Product definition

**ClubsHub** is a centralized university platform connecting students with clubs,
campus activities, announcements, and lost-and-found services.

This Stage 1 document defines the product direction and responsive experience. It
does not authorize authentication, data persistence, APIs, or business workflows.

## Confirmed requirements

- Students need one discoverable place for campus communities and activities.
- The initial navigation must account for Home, Discover Clubs, Events, Lost & Found,
  Announcements, My Activities, and Profile.
- The experience must be mobile-first, accessible, and appropriate for an
  institution.
- Future administrative experiences must be planned without being implemented here.

## Assumptions requiring validation

- The university will provide a trusted student identity source.
- Clubs will have a designated administrator and a review process.
- Events and lost-and-found reports will require moderation.
- Students will prefer a unified experience over separate club channels.

These are product assumptions, not user research findings. No interviews or usability
studies are claimed.

## Goals and value

ClubsHub should reduce the effort required to find belonging, make campus activity
visible, and make community care easier. Success measures will be defined after
stakeholder validation and Stage 2 data architecture.

## Primary users

1. **Students** discover communities, plan around events, follow campus updates, and
   seek help returning lost items.
2. **Club administrators** will eventually present their club, coordinate activity,
   and communicate with members.
3. **Super administrators** will eventually steward platform governance, moderation,
   and institutional oversight.
